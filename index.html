The life

From a poor Boston boyhood
to the heart of the games industry.

Raised in Massachusetts by an Italian-American mother and a German father, Christopher learned early what hard work could build. That drive carried him through America’s oldest public school, into Harvard, and eventually across the Atlantic to build Sony’s games business in Europe from nothing.

1945Born in Boston

Born January 15 in Boston, a working-class start — a family without a TV or car for their first fourteen years. His father managed Harvard’s dining halls.

1960sBoston Latin School & the Math X

Attended Boston Latin School, the oldest free public school in the United States. He was one of thirty-two in the “Math X” section — thirteen of whom won places at Harvard, four at MIT.

 Boston College & Harvard

Studied computer science at Boston College, graduating magna cum laude, then took an MBA in marketing from Harvard Business School.

1970s–80sThe marketer’s apprenticeship

Product manager for the Gillette razor brand, then McKinsey & Co in New York, returning to Gillette to run worldwide shaving before marketing for all of Europe. His first games-industry step: VP of international marketing at Atari, then VP at Spinnaker Software.

1985–95Columbia Pictures & Sony Pictures

Head of international marketing at Columbia Pictures, which Sony acquired in 1989. He rose to international COO of Sony Pictures Home Entertainment from 1990–95.

1995He moves to London — and founds an era

Sent to London to establish Sony Computer Entertainment Europe, serving first as its founding president and later chairman, until 2005. He launched the PlayStation across Europe in 1995, PlayStation 2 in 2000, and PSP in 2004.

2003BAFTA honours him

Received the BAFTA Interactive Industry Award for lifetime contributions to the games industry — alongside an ELSPA Hall of Fame honour and the chairmanship of the Edinburgh Interactive Entertainment Festival.

2006–onA boardroom elder statesman

After retiring from Sony in December 2005, he remained a guiding force — non-executive chairman at Codemasters, director at Wayfinder Systems and PlaySpan, and chairman of Tangentix, with board roles at Geomerics and Jetix Europe.
